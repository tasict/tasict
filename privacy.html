Privacy policy

This policy covers www.ctsite.cc. Last updated October 13, 2026.

What this site collects

The site has no accounts, forms, comments or analytics, and it sets no cookies of its own. It is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Fonts, icons and styles are served from this site, so loading the page does not contact any font or icon service.

Advertising

The home page shows ads from Google AdSense.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Google explains how it uses data from sites that show its ads in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Links to other sites

Product pages, source code, the Chrome Web Store and the support pages are on other sites, each with its own privacy policy.

The products

This policy covers the website, not the products listed on it. Each product describes how it handles data on its own product page, and Pigeon Post for Gmail has a separate privacy policy.

The blog at blog.ctsite.cc is hosted on Blogger and has its own privacy policy.

Questions and changes

Questions about this policy can go to the site’s issue tracker. When the policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
