About ctsite.cc

ctsite.cc is the home of the free, open-source software written and maintained by CT Tsai (tasict on GitHub): Homebridge plugins that bring appliances into Apple Home, a download manager for QNAP NAS, and a Gmail notifier for Chrome.

What the products are for

Most of the products solve the same problem: a device you already own works with its maker’s app but not with Apple Home. The plugins run on Homebridge, an open-source server you host on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any always-on computer at home. Homebridge presents each device to the Home app, so it can be controlled from the Home app and with Siri, and take part in scenes and automations alongside your other accessories.

The two tools outside Apple Home follow the same idea of working with what is already there. Download Center runs on the QNAP NAS itself, so downloads carry on with your computer switched off. Pigeon Post for Gmail uses the Gmail accounts already signed in to Chrome, with no extra sign-in, no OAuth and no server.

How the plugins are built

  • Local control where the device allows it. Daikin Local Platform talks to each air conditioner directly on your network, with no cloud in between. Roborock Vacuum keeps local control when the Roborock cloud is down, and Panasonic Smart App uses the local network with the cloud as a fallback. MQTT Thing EX talks only to your own MQTT broker.
  • HomeKit and Matter. Roborock Vacuum and Daikin Local Platform can expose their devices over Matter as well as HomeKit.
  • Appliances sold in Taiwan. Jci Hitachi Platform works with Hitachi air conditioners from the AirCloud Home app (Hitachi Taiwan), and Panasonic Smart App with appliances from Panasonic Smart App (Taiwan). Both are reviewed by the Homebridge team and listed as verified plugins.
  • Existing setups keep working. MQTT Thing EX is a maintained TypeScript successor to homebridge-mqttthing and reads an existing configuration unchanged.

Every product is released under an open-source license (MIT, Apache-2.0 or GPL-3.0, listed with each product on the home page), and its full source code is on GitHub.

Articles about each product

The ctsite.cc blog has a longer article for each product, written in Traditional Chinese: the problem it solves, how it connects to the device, what it can do in the Home app, how to set it up and what to know before you start. The blog also carries development notes on work in progress.

  • Roborock Vacuum: local control when the cloud is down, and choosing HomeKit or Matter for each vacuum.
  • Daikin Local Platform: controlling Daikin air conditioners entirely on the home network, the supported Wi-Fi adapters, and the Matter mode.
  • Jci Hitachi Platform: how the plugin reaches the air conditioner through AirCloud Home, frost wash, and using a main or shared account.
  • Panasonic Smart App: how the plugin finds your appliances, and local control with the cloud as a fallback.
  • MQTT Thing EX: configuring a light, the device types it covers, and moving over from homebridge-mqttthing.
  • Download Center: links, torrents and magnet links in one queue on the NAS, and notifications you can reply to.
  • Pigeon Post for Gmail: unread counts for every signed-in account, choosing which mail matters, and where the data goes.
  • Turnout: development notes on a macOS VPN client for WireGuard servers that lets you choose which network interface the traffic uses.

Getting help

Each product has its own issue tracker on GitHub, linked from its product page. That is the place for bug reports, questions and feature requests, so the answer is there for the next person with the same question. A report is easiest to act on when it says which version you run and includes the relevant part of the log.

For anything about this website itself, open an issue on the site’s issue tracker.

Supporting the work

The products are free. The home page of this site and the blog show Google AdSense ads, which the privacy policy describes. If a product is useful to you, you can also support its development with a bubble tea (paid by card, no PayPal account needed) or through PayPal.
