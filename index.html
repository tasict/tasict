Homebridge plugins for Apple Home

Bring Daikin, Hitachi and Panasonic appliances, Roborock robot vacuums and MQTT devices into the Home app, with Siri, scenes and automations. Each plugin runs on Homebridge, which you can host on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any always-on computer at home. All five are free and open source.

  • Roborock Vacuum

    Control Roborock robot vacuums from the Home app over HomeKit or Matter. Start a clean with Siri or an automation, and keep local control when the Roborock cloud is down.

    Works with
    HomeKit, Matter
    Connection
    Local network, cloud sign-in
    Price
    Free, open source (MIT)

    Product page Source code Article (Chinese)

  • Daikin Local Platform

    Control Daikin air conditioners from the Home app over HomeKit or Matter. The plugin talks to each unit directly on your network, with no cloud in between.

    Works with
    HomeKit, Matter
    Connection
    Local network only
    Price
    Free, open source (Apache-2.0)

    Product page Source code Article (Chinese)

  • Jci Hitachi Platform

    Verified by Homebridge
