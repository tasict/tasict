Bring the devices you already own into Apple Home.

Homebridge plugins for Daikin, Hitachi and Panasonic appliances, Roborock robot vacuums and MQTT devices. Control them from the Home app, with Siri, scenes and automations, and on your own network wherever the device allows it.

Alongside them: a download manager for QNAP NAS and a Gmail notifier for Chrome. Every ctsite.cc product is free and open source.

Homebridge plugins

Each plugin runs on Homebridge, which you can host on a Raspberry Pi, a NAS or any always-on computer at home.

  • Roborock Vacuum

    Control Roborock robot vacuums from the Home app over HomeKit or Matter. Start a clean with Siri or an automation, and keep local control when the Roborock cloud is down.

    Works with
    HomeKit, Matter
    Connection
    Local network, cloud sign-in
    License
    MIT

  • Daikin Local Platform

    Control Daikin air conditioners from the Home app over HomeKit or Matter. The plugin talks to each unit directly on your network, with no cloud in between.
